Asset lifecycle — full stakeholder journey
Stage 1 — Manufacturer
Product manufactured · GTIN registered · EPD commissioned
ALUPROF SA registers MB-45 and MB-78EI GTINs with GS1. Constable Doors provides FSC certified timber doors with EPD. Carbon data: A1–A3 cradle to gate.
Stage 2 — Designer
Product specified in BIM · DPP parameters added to Revit families
GS1 GTIN, EPD reference, embodied carbon, material composition, Declaration of Performance added as shared parameters to Revit door and window families. COBie template extended to include DPP fields.
Stage 3 — Contractor (Sisk)
DPP data verified at technical submittal · IFC exported to Dalux CDE
Sisk verifies supplier GTIN against GS1 registry at ITT stage. Enriched IFC exported from Revit with all DPP parameters. Uploaded to Dalux CDE under EU replacement folder. GS1 Digital Link QR code generated and affixed to physical asset on site.
Stage 4 — Installation
QR code scanned on site · Physical asset linked to AIM record
Site team scans GS1 Digital Link QR code on door or window. Scan resolves to this DPP record — confirming correct product installed, carbon data recorded, compliance verified at point of installation.
Stage 5 — Handover
COBie exported with DPP fields · AIM handed over to client
Full COBie output includes DPP parameters. Dalux AIM handed to client FM team. Golden Thread requirement satisfied under UK BSA 2022. EU ESPR DPP mandate fulfilled.
Stage 6 — Operation · FM Team
Scan-to-record for maintenance · End of life data accessible
FM team scans QR code during maintenance visits. Full asset history, warranty status, and maintenance schedule retrieved instantly. At end of building life — material composition and recycling classification enables circular economy decisions.
Standard vs bespoke product pathways
Standard pathway
Off-the-shelf product
  • 01GTIN already registered with GS1 registry
  • 02Sisk mandates GTIN in ITT documentation
  • 03Supplier provides GTIN + EPD at technical submittal
  • 04Sisk enters data into Revit shared parameters
  • 05COBie exports DPP data into Dalux AIM
  • 06GS1 Digital Link QR affixed to physical asset
Bespoke pathway
Custom manufactured product
  • 01No GTIN exists — custom product
  • 02Sisk mandates DPP data in ITT — extended lead time
  • 03Manufacturer registers new GTIN with GS1
  • 04Manufacturer commissions product-specific EPD
  • 05Manufacturer establishes DPP hosting URL
  • 06Then follows standard pathway above

GS1 Digital Link QR Code

Scan this QR code on site with any mobile device to access the full Digital Product Passport record for this asset. No proprietary app required — uses open GS1 standard (ISO/IEC 18004).

How GS1 Digital Link works
Step 1 — Physical asset
QR code affixed to door or window on site
Laser-etched stainless steel plate recommended for durability
Step 2 — Scan
Any phone camera scans the QR code
No proprietary app required — works with native camera
Step 3 — Resolution
URL resolves simultaneously to BIM element, COBie record, DPP data, EPD and O&M documents
Step 4 — DPP record
Full asset data appears instantly
Available for minimum 10 years after installation per EU ESPR
